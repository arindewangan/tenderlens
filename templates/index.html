Find the government tenders your business can actually win.

TenderLens aggregates Indian public-sector tenders from GeM, CPPP and state e-procurement portals — discovered live via SerpApi — extracts the EMD, deadlines and values that matter, and scores each tender against your business profile.

Set up my business profile → Browse all tenders

How it works

  1. Discover. SerpApi's Google Search API runs site-restricted queries across India's tender portals — search data is the core of the app.
  2. Extract. EMD, bid due dates, estimated values, authority, location and category are parsed from every listing.
  3. Match. Each tender is scored 0–100 against your sector, turnover band, locations and certifications — with reasons, not just a number.
  4. Brief. Plain-English briefs explain why a tender matters and what to check before you bid.
